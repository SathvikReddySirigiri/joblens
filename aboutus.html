About Us

The team behind JobLens.

Our mission

Making the job search safer for every student.

JobLens helps students spot fake recruiters and risky job offers before they reply. We combine domain checks, community scam reports, and an AI assistant so career centers can protect students and employers can protect their brand.

  • 🛡️ Verify before you reply
  • 🤝 Community-powered reports
  • 📊 Data-driven insights
