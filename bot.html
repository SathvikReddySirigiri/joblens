What Sentinel knows

Sentinel answers from the ScamPatterns table: 12 recruiter-scam patterns students actually run into. Each pattern includes how the scam works, red flags, an example message, a risk level, and what to do next.

  • Payment scams: fake checks, equipment “reimbursement,” upfront fees, and visa or sponsorship fees
  • Impersonation: look-alike domains, personal email addresses, chat-only interviews, and fake staffing agencies
  • Identity theft and fake jobs: requests for your SSN or bank details, package reshipping, and high-pay data entry
